Features of KE-430DP

Clean Sewing with high speed

It is a Semi-dry type. Clean oil is supplied to only the hook. No oil is used around the needle bar and thread take-up with maximum speed up to 3,200sti/min.

Easy handling

Easy machine set-up by placing control box on the head.
