 3-16. Cautions for the compressed air supply (source of supply air) facility
As large as 90 % of failures in pneumatic equipment (air cylinders, air solenoid valves) are caused by
"contaminated air."
Compressed air contains lots of impurities such as moisture, dust, deteriorated oil and carbon parti-
cles. If such "contaminated air" is used without taking any measures, it can a cause of troubles, inviting
reduction in productivity due to mechanical failures and reduced availability.
Be sure to install the standard air supply facility shown below whenever the machine provided with
pneumatic equipment is used.

          Cautions for main piping
         • Be sure to slope main piping by a falling gradient of 1 cm per 1 m in the direction of air flow.
