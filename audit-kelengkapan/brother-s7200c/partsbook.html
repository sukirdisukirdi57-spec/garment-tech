[How to search using an illustration]

  • 1.Click on the "Search" button.
  • 2.Click on the "MODEL" button.
  • 3.Click on the "Mechanism" button of the sewing machine that you want parts for.
  • 4.Click on the "Detail" button of the mechanism that includes the desired parts.
  • 5.Select desired parts using either of the following methods.
    Click on the part code of the part you are looking for in the illustration onthe left.
    Click on the part name or part code of the part you are looking for in the parts list on theright.
  • 6. Click on the check box of the part you are looking for in the partslist on the right.
  • 7. Repeat steps 3 to 6 above to put check marks in the boxes of all the parts you are looking for.
  • 8. When wanting to select parts of another component or assembly, clickon the "Model name" .
    When wanting to select a part from another model, click on the "Model" button.
  • 9.Click on the "SELECTED PARTS" button.
  • 10.To download your list, click on the "DOWNLOAD" button.

[How to search by part code]

  • 1. Click on the "PARTS" button.
  • 2. Enter the part code, then click the “Search” button.
  • 3. Click on the check box of the part you are looking for.
  • 4. Repeat steps 2 to 3 above to put check marks in the boxes of all the parts you are looking for.
  • 5. Click on the "SELECTED PARTS" button.
  • 6. To download your list, click on the "DOWNLOAD" button.
